How to use this checklist

Check things off

Tap an item when it's done. Tap it again to uncheck it.

Tap a section's title to fold it away.

Split the jobs

Under Filter pick Outside or Inside to see only your half. Everything shows the whole list.

Name your travel day

Tap Name this trip at the top and type a name, like Key West > Anchorage. Tap the name to change it.

Make it fit your trailer

  1. Tap Customize list.
  2. Tap Hide on anything your trailer doesn't have.
  3. Add your own items at the bottom of any section.

"If equipped" items only apply to some trailers.

Share with your travel partner

Both phones see the same list, and every checkmark shows up on both. No sign-up.

  1. Tap Share list, then Share my list.
  2. Tap Send the link to my partner and text it to them.
  3. Your partner taps the link, then Join.

Using the home screen icon? Open it, tap Join my partner's list (or Share list first if a list is already going), and type the 6-letter code.

Tip: Joining replaces the items the joining phone added itself. Let whoever has the custom items tap Share my list.

Start a new travel day

Tap Start a new travel day at the bottom. It unchecks everything and keeps your own items.

Bigger text and dark mode

Open the Menu and tap Text size to make the words bigger. Dark mode is easier on the eyes at night.

Use it like an app

Put it on your home screen (steps below). It works with no signal, and the screen stays on while you check items off.

Is my information private?

No sign-up, no ads, no tracking. Your list stays on your phone. If you share it, the list and its code are also kept on our sync service so both phones can see it. No names or emails are collected, and unused lists are deleted after a year.

Put this on your home screen

iPhone or iPad

  1. Open this page in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow pointing up).
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen, then tap Add.

Android

  1. Open this page in Chrome.
  2. Tap the three dots in the top corner.
  3. Tap Add to Home screen, then tap Add.
