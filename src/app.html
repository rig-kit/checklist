How to use this checklist

Check off every step of hitching up and packing up, so you hit the road with peace of mind.

Check things off

Tap an item when it's done. After a moment it moves to Completed at the bottom, so the list keeps getting shorter.

Checked one by mistake? Tap it in Completed and it goes back.

Tap a section's title to fold it away.

Filter the list

Under Filter pick Outside or Inside to filter the checklist items. Choose Everything to see the full list.

Name your travel day

Tap Name this trip at the top and type a name, like Jackson to Flagstaff. Tap the name to change it.

Customize your own list

  1. Tap Customize list.
  2. Tap Hide on anything your trailer doesn't have.
  3. Use the up and down arrows to put items, or whole sections, in the order you work.
  4. Add your own items at the bottom of any section.

To see everything you've added, hidden, or moved, open the Menu and tap My custom and hidden items.

"If equipped" items only apply to some trailers.

Share with your travel partner

Both phones see the same list, and every checkmark shows up on both. No sign-up.

  1. Tap Share list, then Share my list.
  2. Tap Send the link to my partner and text it to them.
  3. Your partner taps the link, then Join.

Using the home screen icon? Open it, tap Join my partner's list (or Share list first if a list is already going), and type the 6-letter code.

Tip: Joining replaces the items the joining phone added itself. Let whoever has the custom items tap Share my list.

Start a new travel day

Tap Start a new travel day at the top of the checklist. It unchecks everything and keeps your own items. Once everything is checked, a Start next travel day button also shows at the bottom.

If you share with your partner, the list stays shared, so their phone starts the new travel day too. No need to share again.

Bigger text and dark mode

Open the Menu and tap Text size to make the words bigger. Dark mode is easier on the eyes at night.

Add this app to your home screen

Put it on your home screen: open the Menu and tap Put this on your home screen. It works with no signal, and the screen stays on while you check items off.

Who makes this?

Full-time Airstreamers who built the list we use on our own travel days. We're independent and not connected to Airstream, Inc. Always follow your owner's manuals too.

Is my information private?

No sign-up, no ads, no tracking. Your list stays on your phone. We keep a simple tally of how often the checklist is opened and used, so we know if it's helping people. It holds counts only, with no cookies and nothing that identifies you or your phone. If you share it, the list and its code are also kept on our sync service so both phones can see it. No names or emails are collected, and unused lists are deleted after a year.
